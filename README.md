## 💡 Strategic Insights & Key Findings

- **US Geographic Moat**: US-based tech enterprises maintain a 60-120% pay premium over comparable European and Asian roles.
- **ML Engineering Premium**: Specializing in Machine Learning Infrastructure yields 18-24% higher compensation than standard BI analytics.
- **Remote Equalization**: Fully remote roles across US employers have normalized national compensation bands.
- **Large vs Small Firms**: Medium-sized scaleups (50-250 employees) offer competitive base pay + higher equity upside than corporate monoliths.
